# Playtest: Plum vs Spearmint, room 1 (2026-10-03)

A fresh seat (Plum, SE corner, 272x272 map, live `rules_version` 5) played over the HTTP API against Spearmint, a
seasoned turtle driven by `~/.pezz-bot/bot.py`. Spearmint had 25k steel banked, 4 laser towers, 8 commandos and 4
artillery. Plum won after about 52 minutes and four assault waves. The first three failed. The fourth was an
infantry-heavy wave that went in once the defender had spent its circuits rebuilding.

Final Plum stats: 78 kills, 71 units lost, 0 structures lost.

## P0: live is stuck on an old build

- `ci-work/status.json` reads "waiting: first resumable deploy waits for room 1 to empty". The running build
  (rules v5) can't save games, so `arena/deploy.sh` exits 3 every 3 minutes. Main (v9: `prospect`, `drill`,
  inventions, the `deep_mine` map glyph) never ships.
- Every one of those attempts still broadcasts "⏸ Server maintenance in a moment" to all players, because
  `deploy.sh` step 1 (notice) runs before step 2 finds out it can't save. Spearmint's event log held 25 of these
  in a row. Agents learn to ignore the notice. Fix: check that the build can save before step 1.
- Room 1 never empties. Any seated player blocks the deploy, and `bot.py` auto-rejoins as a new seat the moment
  it's eliminated (it came straight back as Blueberry). Consider letting idle or bot seats not block a swap, or
  have `ci.sh` deploy `--fresh` after N hours of waiting.

## Bugs

- **`get_map` crashes once you own a deep mine** (live v5): "The given key 'deep_mine' was not present in the
  dictionary". Already fixed on main (`Q`).
- **Live server rejects `prospect` and `drill`** ("unknown command type"), but the MCP tool description and
  `whats_new` both advertise them. Docs are ahead of the server (same root cause as P0).
- **A survey aimed at a rock tile hangs forever.** The surveyor parks next to the rock with order `survey` and
  never surveys or fails. It should survey from the nearest reachable tile, or error.
- **Fog isn't mutual.** An enemy gun turret (sight 7) saw my artillery 6.8 tiles away, but my artillery (also sight
  7) didn't see the turret. Likely a tile-corner vs centre distance mismatch.
- **The salvage alert overstates the spill.** "STOCKPILE SPILLED … 31439 units", but only ~6.9k ever appeared as ore
  fields (iron 3892, plus 1000 each of uranium, copper and crystal).
- **`sell` on a deep mine** returns "sold deep_mine for " (empty refund).
- **Stale alerts never expire.** Spearmint's state led with 25 PRIORITY ALERTS from 14,000–17,000 s earlier.
- **Outpost lands somewhere else.** The truck was reported at (157,87) when I ordered `deploy`; the outpost appeared
  at (141,95).

## Engine and API gaps that made play harder

**Economy**
- **Raw-ore costs become unpayable.** Power plant, refinery and mining truck cost raw `iron_ore`/`copper_ore`.
  Surface ore near a new base was gone in about 2 minutes (copper in about 60 s). After that, refineries convert
  deep-mine ore as fast as it arrives, so raw ore never banks. To afford a second refinery I had to sell one,
  then drill a second copper mine just to overflow the first. Wanted: a hold/pause toggle per converter, or let
  steel pay for these.
- **Deep mines die all at once, with no warning.** Deposits of 4–7k at 4/s last 17–30 minutes, and my three iron
  mines ran dry within minutes of each other. Wanted: a "runs dry in ~Xs" field on each mine, and a heads-up alert
  ~2 minutes ahead.
- **`harvest any` with no known ore** reports "2 truck(s) mining", then the trucks go idle at once. Say why
  ("no ore known: scout or survey").

**Fuel and logistics**
- **Cross-map attacks silently need an outpost chain.** Artillery has a 260-tile tank; the enemy was 337 tiles away.
  The army auto-turned home at ~46% fuel (the bingo rule: fuel left ≈ distance to the nearest pad) and I learned
  this mid-march. I needed two outposts roughly every 100–120 tiles. Wanted: a fuel/ETA check on `move` ("will turn
  back at ~(170,80)"), and a convoy repair truck that counts as a pad for the bingo rule. Repair trucks did refuel
  units in the field, which helped.
- **Units burn fuel driving to a rally point.** With the rally set at the forward outpost, units arrived half-empty.
- **Drones don't manage their own fuel.** One hovered idle 5 tiles from the factory until it was down to 5%.
  Another ran down to 30% mid-route with no auto-return. Vehicles do return on their own; drones should too, and
  an idle drone should land.

**Control**
- **`train` can't pick which barracks or factory.** I sold my home barracks so riflemen would come out of the
  forward one. Add an optional `structure_id`.
- **No spread or formation option.** Artillery splash shredded my clumped riflemen. Something like `"spread":true`
  on `move` / `attack_move` would help.
- **`get_rules` never mentions sight.** Artillery has range 11 but sight 7, so it can't fire at full range without
  a spotter. I found that in `Defs.cs`. Put sight in rules and in each unit's state.
- **`get_map` has no x-axis tens digits**, so it's hard to find a column on a large window.
- **MCP allows one seat per session.** I couldn't join a second seat ("already playing") and fell back to HTTP.

**Things that worked well**
- `attack` on a remembered but unseen target: "use attack_move toward its last known position". Clear and
  actionable.
- Auto-refuel then resume the order; `together:true`; forward barracks and outposts as build anchors.
- `build_options` with `short_of` and `why` made automation easy.

## Balance observations

- **A commando turtle is very strong against vehicles.** C4 does 900 × 0.6 = 540 to vehicles, which one-shots
  artillery (300 hp) and two-shots heavy tanks. Three vehicle-heavy waves died to 8 commandos, 4 artillery and the
  laser towers.
- **The counter works but needs the right timing.** Infantry are immune to C4, and the defender's real limit was
  circuits (1085, then 354, then 70 after rebuilding). Once it couldn't replace artillery, riflemen plus rocket
  soldiers plus artillery broke it: 16 riflemen, 9 rockets, 2 medics and 6 artillery killed 4 artillery, 9
  commandos and 4 laser towers in about 2 minutes.
- **A 25k banked steel stockpile meant little, because circuits gated everything.** That's good design, but
  nothing hints at it to the attacker.
- **An open arena never declares a winner** (by design, `World.cs`). Spearmint sat as sole survivor for hours with
  no acknowledgement on the live build.

## Status of the findings (rules_version 10)

| Finding | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance notice when no deploy happens | `arena/deploy.sh` saves first; a build that can't save exits 3 before any notice |
| Room 1 never empties, CI waits forever | `/api/lobby` reports each seat's `idle_s`; `ci.sh` and `swap-build-when-empty.sh` ignore seats idle for `PEZZ_CI_IDLE_S` (30 min); after `PEZZ_CI_MAX_WAIT_S` (3 h) of waiting, `ci.sh` starts the new build fresh anyway (players get the usual restart notice) |
| `get_map` crash on `deep_mine`; `prospect`/`drill` rejected | Already fixed on main; live picks them up once the deploy goes through |
| Survey aimed at rock hangs | Already fixed on main (surveys from up to 3 tiles off, else says why) |
| Fog asymmetry | A tile is in view when sight reaches any part of it, so a unit sees a structure from as far as the structure sees it |
| Salvage alert overstated | The spill covers the footprint, then open ground around it, until all of it lands; alerts report the amount placed |
| `sell` on a free structure | "removed … it cost nothing, so there's no refund; N power freed" |
| Stale alerts on rejoin | New-alert lists skip alerts quiet for more than 2 minutes |
| Outpost lands elsewhere | Not a bug (the truck kept rolling); `deploy` now names the structure and where it stands |
| Raw-ore costs unpayable | `{"type":"reserve","item":"iron_ore","amount":300}`: converters leave that much alone; cost errors suggest it; `reserves` in state |
| Deep mines die without warning | `runs_dry_in_s` per mine in state; DEEP DEPOSIT RUNNING LOW alert at 2 minutes left |
| `harvest any` with no ore | The result names the trucks that found none, and why |
| No fuel check on long marches | `move`/`attack_move` warn NOT ENOUGH FUEL with where units would turn back; `rally` says what the drive costs; a repair truck in the group covers it |
| Drones burning fuel while idle | An idle aircraft within 12 tiles of a pad lands on it |
| `train` can't pick the building | `structure_id` on `train` |
| No spread command | `"spread":3` (1–6, or true) on `move`/`attack_move` |
| Sight undocumented | `sight` in `get_rules` and on every unit; weapons that outrange their sight say they need a spotter |
| Map columns hard to read | Rulers show the full x number every tenth column; the gateway crop no longer drops the ruler |
| One seat per MCP session | `join` with `switch_seat:true` returns `previous_token` to `rejoin` the first team |
| No winner in an open arena | By design; the arena-cleared milestone on main announces it, and `/api/lobby` now reports `champion` |
