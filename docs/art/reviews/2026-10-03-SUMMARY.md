## Ranked plan: impact over cost, view-only, inside the sim's timings

**Small (hours each)**

1. **Wrecks read as dead.** Fade to 30% over 0.5 s on `destroyed`, team stripe included. The turret stays on the ground, and smoke lasts 12 s.
2. **Ore colour in the truck's load.** Match `M_Ore_*`, tint on `CargoType` change; crystal and uranium glow.
3. **Fire from the real barrel tip.** Compute the tip from the barrel mesh, start shells there, and blend them onto the sim path within 0.1 tiles.
4. **Power you can see.** Power plant cores follow the load (0.6–1.0), flicker at 4 Hz on low power, and steam rises. Consumers dim and animate at half speed on low power.
5. **Damage states on finished buildings.** Smoke below 50% health, fire below 25%. Sites under construction are excluded, since they're low-HP by design.
6. **The refinery gets ready for the truck.** When a loaded truck is 2.5 tiles out, the dock lamps start a 2 Hz chase, the door rolls up and the chute extends; when it's clear, the lamps turn cream.

**Medium (about a day each)**

7. **Construction as an interaction.** The site is staked out as soon as it's placed. The crane turns to it and a beam builds it in stages, with dust as each stage lands. Construction gets its own bar style, not the low-health amber. Everything is driven by build progress, so low power slows it automatically.
8. **The dump as a 1.5 s performance.** The truck pivots so its bin faces the bay, the bed tips, ore bricks and dust pour down the chute, the chassis rises as the load leaves, and the bed slams down. Delight detail: one stuck brick pops out late.
9. **Factory roll-out with anticipation.** Predicted from queue progress: lamps at −1.5 s, a stack vent at −0.8 s, the door clunks at −0.5 s and is open before the unit appears, and the unit eases out of a dark bay. Needs a small `factory.glb` change for the bay.
10. **Mass in motion.** Distinct hull springs per weight class. The braking dip starts about 0.3 s before the sim stops the vehicle, so stopping and firing don't cancel out. The heavy tank fires its barrels alternately. Artillery deploys, loads and fires within its 3.5 s cooldown.
11. **Idle trucks look idle.** Trucks parked because surface ore has run out park and power down, which also signals "go deep".
12. **Mined-out fields fade.** The ground stain fades as a field empties.

**New art (days each; a brief per asset, for the art pipeline)**

- **Artillery hull:** today it's a light tank with a different barrel. It needs spades, a recoil cradle and a longer gun.
- **Mining truck rework:** open hopper, small offset cab, tracks, toothed cutter, and the triangle budget the brief allows.
- **Command center:** an ore intake grate (today ore goes in through its vehicle door), and a crane rebuilt as the tallest thing in the base, on its own footing.
- **A hero form per key building:** for example, power plant coils that compress with load.

The foundations and driveways from the render pass stay: they read at stream scale and come from function. The bevels help only close up, and on their own they push buildings toward the generic slabs §17 warns about. The real gains need the hero forms above.

## Not recommended

- **Making trucks reverse into the dock in the sim.** It adds at least 0.5 s per trip and changes the economy rate, which is a rules decision. The view-side pivot gets the same look.
