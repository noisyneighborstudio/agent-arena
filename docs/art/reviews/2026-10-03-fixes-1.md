# Art fixes, batch 1 (items 1–6 of the ranked plan)

Branch `art/fixes-1`. Plan: [2026-10-03-SUMMARY.md](2026-10-03-SUMMARY.md). Everything here is view-only: nothing in `unity/Assets/Pez/Sim` changed on this branch (the truck bay steps it uses came from main, rules_version 10), and `headless --test` passes after every commit.

Each item has its own preview in the gallery (`https://<host>.ts.net:8452/pezz-previews.html`). The default stills (overview, both bases, the stream) come from a fresh 2-AI game; the `fixN-*` stills and videos are staged moments from a test copy, because a 3-minute fresh game rarely shows a wreck, a low-power base or a burning building.

## Shared mechanism: per-renderer view states

`Pez/Model` gained two instanced properties, set through `PezShade` (one shared `MaterialPropertyBlock`, no allocation):

- `_PezDim` darkens albedo, specular and emission, and mattes the surface (wrecks).
- `_PezGlow` scales emission only (power plant cores, consumers on low power, dock lamps).

Both are in the instancing buffer, so instanced batching keeps working. Property blocks are only rewritten when a value changes.

## 1. Wrecks read as dead

- **Trigger:** the `destroyed` event (`EV.Destroyed`), in `PezEmerge.PlayRemove(destroyed: true)`.
- **What happens:**
  - Every renderer of the wreck, team stripe and head included, darkens over 0.5 s (ease-out). MOTION.md's "30%" is read as 30% as seen, which is 0.08 in linear light. At 0.3 linear the team colour still read as a bright stripe on the stream.
  - The head is blown off as before (up 2.5, sideways about 1, tumbling), bounces once and settles on its base beside the hull, cocked 8–22°. It stays part of the wreck: dark, and it sinks with the hull after 20 s.
  - Wreck smoke lasts 12 s (was 4.5 s), at a lower rate (`Fx.WreckSmoke`).
- **Bench:** `-fxdemo wrecks` shows live tanks beside fresh and older wrecks.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 1", `fix1-wrecks.jpg` (close, and at 40 px/tile).

## 2. The truck's load shows its ore

- **Bug fixed:** `Models.TintOre` matched only `M_OreTint`; the truck's `bin_ore` is `M_Ore_Cinnamon`, so every load showed as iron.
- **Now:** `TintOre` matches `M_Ore_*` too and maps tinted copies back to their original, so re-tinting never chains materials. The truck re-tints when `CargoType` changes and keeps the last colour while it empties.
- **Glow:** crystal and uranium loads glow with the field's own emissive (laser cyan, acid), not their paler albedo, so a load reads as the stuff in the field.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 2", `fix2-ore-loads.jpg`.

## 3. Fire from the real barrel tip

- **Muzzle:** `Models.Build` caches each art-pack barrel's muzzle: the middle of the far +Z end of its meshes, in barrel space. Every barrel in the pack points along +Z: light tank 0.525, heavy 0.69, artillery 0.85 on a node pitched 60°, laser and SAM racks to their emitter faces. `MuzzleOf` returns that point, so it follows turret yaw, barrel pitch and recoil.
- **What uses it:** muzzle flashes, tracers, and repair and heal beams.
- **Shells** start at the tip in 3D. Artillery now leaves its raised barrel about 1.1 up, not 0.3. Across the ground, the shell joins the sim's path within 0.12 tiles of flight.
- **Truck bay, from main:** in the same preview, trucks back into the bay (rules_version 10). The bed now follows the sim's steps: it tips during Unload's settle and lowers over PullOut's first 0.25 s.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 3: barrel tips + trucks back into the bay", `fix3-muzzle.jpg`, and `fix3-trucks-back-in.jpg` (lining up, backed in, unloading with the next truck waiting).

## 4. Power you can see

- **Trigger:** the team's `PowerUsed`, `PowerProduced` and `LowPower`, read every frame.
- **Power plant cores:** emission 0.6–1.0 with load (`used / produced`), eased over 0.4 s, and dark until the plant is complete. On low power they flicker at 4 Hz between 0.25 and 1, with a 0.1 s dropout every 2–3 s. The core body darkens with its light, so the off beat reads.
- **Steam:** cream wisps rise from both towers, one per `0.8 / load` s each (`Fx.Steam`, on the Dust system). On low power it sputters: bursts of 3, then a 1 s gap.
- **Consumers** (`Power < 0`): on low power their `M_E_*` emissives drop to 50%, and their spinner, door, lift and beam run at half speed (`PezMotion.SetRate`), as the sim halves production. Turrets and recoil are unchanged, since low power doesn't slow defences.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 4", `fix4-power.jpg` (a low-power base with the flicker on and off, and a healthy base).

## 5. Damage states on finished buildings

- **Trigger:** `IsComplete && Hp / MaxHp < 0.5` for smoke, `< 0.25` for fire. Construction sites never show it, because their HP rises with build progress.
- **Smoke:** a column from the roof at 3 puffs/s, thickening to 7/s below 25%.
- **Fire:** flame licks, an occasional ember, and the fire's light flickering on the ground as a light pool. No per-pixel light, so it reads in every view and stream and doesn't take explosion lamps.
- **Timing:** fades in over 0.5 s and out over 2 s, so repairs clear it.
- **Cost:** reuses the Smoke, Fire, Sparks and Pool systems. Roof bounds are measured once per building.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 5", `fix5-damage.jpg` (factory at 42% smoking; refinery at 18% and barracks at 20% burning; a plant at 70% clean; close and at 40 px/tile).

## 6. The drop-off gets ready for the truck

**Trigger.** The sim's bay steps: the delivering truck's `Dock` and `DockAt` (main, rules_version 10). This is exact, so no distance inference was needed. Each frame a truck reports its step to its drop-off (`WorldView.ReportDock`).

| Truck step | Lamps | Door | Chute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Align (about 1 s before it reverses), Reverse, Unload | 2 Hz amber chase | up | slides 0.5 tiles out toward the truck over 0.5 s, ease-out with about 0.03 overshoot |
| PullOut | steady cream ("clear") | up | retracts |
| Bay free | dark 0.6 s later | rolls down 0.5 s later | in |

- **Lamps:** the model's two amber dock lamps (`stage_1__M_E_Amber`; they are two small lamps, not a strip), plus pips built in code. On the refinery and command center, a column of three pips under each lamp chases upward ("come in"). On the outpost's low pad, a row between the lamps chases inward. Off, the lamps are unlit, so the glow means a state.
- **Chute:** the pack has no chute node, so `DockView` builds a kraft trough with licorice lips for the refinery and the command center. Out, its lip sits over the backed-in truck's tail; in, it is parked in the refinery's bay, or behind the command center's door. The outpost's pad takes ore without one.
- **Side bays:** if terrain moved the bay to another side (`World.Bay`), the chute comes out of that wall and the door stays shut. The lamps stay on the door side, because they are part of the model.
- **Lane built over:** a truck unloading beside the drop-off makes the bay active while it tips.
- **Gallery:** "Art fix 6", `fix6-refinery-close.jpg`, `fix6-refinery-gameplay-distance.jpg`, `fix6-command-center.jpg`, and the video `fix6-truck-docks-at-command-center.mp4`.

## Not done, and why

- **Item 6: the chute doesn't engage, and lamps on side bays.** The chute doesn't drop 0.05 onto the bin lip ("clunk"); it's simple to add with item 8's pour. On side bays the lamps stay on the model's door wall.
- **Item 6: the command center door.** At the command center the vehicle door rolls up for ore. The review's R7 (an intake grate) needs new art. Until then the door is the only opening, and the chute comes out of it.
- **Information leaks to player streams.** Particles (steam, damage smoke) are shared by every camera. A player's stream can see steam or smoke rising out of fog over a known enemy building, as it already can for explosions. Per-team particle layers would fix it.

## Deploying into a live, resumed game

- No sim, API, save or rules change on this branch (the bay steps are main's). Everything is computed at runtime.
- On resume, existing wrecks aren't re-created. Structures pick up their power and damage state on the first frame, and drop-offs build their lamps and chute when their view is created.
- New GameObjects per drop-off: 4–6 lamp pips and a chute (3 cubes). There are no new per-frame allocations. Effect particles go into the existing pooled systems.
- `Pez/Model` has two new instanced properties. Any material that doesn't set them uses the defaults (1), so other uses are unchanged.
