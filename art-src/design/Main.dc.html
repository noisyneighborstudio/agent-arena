VISUAL STYLE GUIDE · v0.1 · THE DISPENSER WAR

PEZ

Four flavours go to war over sugar. Armies are built from molded plastic, spring steel and foil: toys engineered like real machines. The world stays unflavoured and muted so that team colour and glow carry the meaning. Every asset must read from 25 units up, where one tile is about 40 px.

04

Shape language: everything dispenses

Stem
The body. Hulls and towers are stems; a window shows the magazine.
Head
Flip-top, hinged, team colour. Heads are turrets, cranes and hatches.
Spring
Visible coil means power or reload. Compressed coil = loaded.
Brick
Rounded candy brick. Ammo, ore, payload and the resource icon base.

Tell the tech apart

Conventional
No glow. Thick steel barrel, brick muzzle. Fires bricks; smoke and sugar dust.
Laser

Plasma
Stubby emitter, magenta ring and core. Soft taffy bolt that wobbles in flight.

08

Rules

Silhouette first
Recognisable from outline alone. Weapons and working parts +20%; infantry weapons and heads +40%.
The world is unflavoured
Terrain and props stay muted: biscuit plains, licorice cliffs, cola water, dusty cotton-candy trees. Saturation belongs to teams and glow.
Gloss tells material
Team plastic is glossy with a hard top-left highlight; steel is brushed; foil is crinkled. Push the contrast between materials, such as gloss plastic beside matte licorice.
70 / 30 surface
70% clean rest areas of flat plastic. 30% detail, made up of team mask (15–25%), glow (≤5%) and grilles. Detail sits on top, not underneath.
Top is brightest
The highest contrast and brightest values sit on top-facing surfaces. Undersides, lower hulls and legs get minimal detail and darker values.
Baked AO, no cast shadows
Bake AO and a soft top-down gradient (+10% at the top, −15% at the base) into albedo, so units stay readable in fog. Never paint directional or cast shadows.
Light
Warm sun from the upper left of the screen, cool sky fill. Shadows fall to the lower right in the off-axis view.
Doors face south
Exits, docks and dispensing slots on −Z, toward the camera.
Pivot and scale
1 tile = 1 unit = 1 m. Pivot at ground, footprint centre. 0.04 margin on every side.
09

Nothing pops

Every spawn, build, deploy, regrowth, sale and death is a transform over time.
0–10%
Pad lifts flush (stage_0)
10–45%
Walls rise through the pad (stage_1)
45–75%
Upper body rises (stage_2)
75–95%
Heads and caps rise, 4% spring overshoot (stage_3)
95–100%
Turrets rise, spinners spin up
Vehicles
Spawn behind the factory door. The door rolls up and the unit drives out 1.5 tiles south.
Infantry
Walk out through the barracks door, one at a time.
Aircraft
Rise on the airfield lift from −0.6, spin up, then climb to cruise altitude.
Ore and removal
Ore regrows from below ground. Destroyed or sold things sink in reverse.
10

Camera

  • Off-axis orthographic: yaw 45°, pitch 55°.
  • Zoom changes the ortho height, from 8 (close) to 40 (strategic).
  • Two faces of every building show, so model both the south and east faces.
  • The minimap stays north-up with sectors A–H and 1–8, because LLM orders use grid coordinates.

13

Buildings and animation

Damage states
Intact, 50% and 25% (scorch decals, broken panels, fire and smoke sockets), then rubble. Rubble keeps the exact footprint so the pathing lines stay readable.
Upgrades add parts
An upgrade bolts on a visible module, such as a second stack, a bigger dish or an extra hopper. It rises like a build stage and never changes colour alone.
Anchored to the ground
Every structure sits on its sugar pad (stage_0) with a dark dirt-skirt decal 0.15 past the footprint. Units get a soft contact-shadow decal.
Snappy combat
Idle to firing within 3 frames. Turret slew and laser charge are deliberate telegraphs; the shot itself is instant. Recoil out ≤ 0.05 s.
Vertex shaders for ambience
Flags, antennas, cotton-candy trees, the crane hook and the hover bob all run in a vertex shader, not on bones or nodes.
Glow shows state
Coils charge before a shot, cores pulse with load and flicker on low power. Glow never just decorates.
