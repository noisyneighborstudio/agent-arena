Rocks and mountains

same camera · built from the rock grid by PezCliffBuilder

Why it fails

No edge
Noise humps fade into the ground, so nobody can see where pathing is blocked.
Spheres, not rock
Round balls scattered on a blob read as bubbles or mould. There are no facets and no silhouette.
Same value as shadow
The grey-purple masses match the ground's dark noise clouds, so rock, shadow and dirt blur together.
No height
Every mass is one low bump. Nothing reads as a mountain at 40 px per tile.

Rules

The dark rim is the blocked line
Licorice walls follow the blocked tiles to within about 0.3 of a tile. The darkest thing on the terrain is a cliff wall.
Thickness becomes height
Thick masses climb 2–3 terraces (1.0, +0.85, +0.7) with uneven widths. The crust lightens on each tier, so the top is brightest. Thin ridges stay as one mesa.
Faceted, never round
Flat-shaded low-poly faces. Boulders come in 3 sizes × 3 shapes, at the wall base and as crumble on the terraces.
Quiet ground
Ground value varies by at most 8%. Large dark noise clouds read as fake shadows and fight both the cliffs and the units.
